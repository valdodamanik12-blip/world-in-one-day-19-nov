THE WORLD IN ONE DAY

THURSDAY 19 NOVEMBER 2026
Three presidents in one room, a genocide verdict in The Hague — and the biggest IPO in history
China — Lead story

TRUMP, XI AND PUTIN HOLD FIRST THREE-WAY SUMMIT

The leaders of the United States, China and Russia meet together for the first time, on the sidelines of the APEC forum in Shenzhen. After a 90-minute session they announce a joint “peace declaration” pledging dialogue on nuclear risks and stable energy flows.

Sources: Associated Press, Just Security, Algenom
Justice — The Hague

COURT: MYANMAR COMMITTED GENOCIDE

The International Court of Justice rules Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in northern Rakhine State — the first such ruling by the U.N.’s highest court.

Middle East — Saudi Arabia

HOUTHI MISSILE HITS RIYADH’S TALLEST TOWER

A Houthi missile damages the PIF Tower, headquarters of Saudi Arabia’s sovereign wealth fund, blowing out windows across seven to eight floors. No injuries are reported.

Markets — Technology

ANTHROPIC DEBUTS IN BIGGEST IPO EVER

The Claude maker starts trading on Nasdaq at $83 a share, raising $81.3 billion — the largest stock-market debut on record — and valuing the company at $1.5 trillion.

Weather — India

CYCLONE AKVAN NEARS INDIA’S EAST COAST

The severe cyclonic storm is set to make landfall near Gopalpur on Friday, with 90–100 km/h winds, a storm-surge warning and extremely heavy rain.

Gaza — Aid

GAZA PEACE PLAN SCORES 4 OUT OF 26

Five aid groups give the U.N.-backed peace plan a failing mark in a joint review, as Israeli forces launch a new operation in central Gaza.

Sources: AP · Reuters · Bloomberg · Just Security · IMD · ICJ All events reported Thursday 19 November 2026
